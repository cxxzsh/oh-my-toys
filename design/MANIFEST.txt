oh-my-toys design system: the rules, tokens, components and assets every screen follows.

README.md                 brand book: rules for every screen
tokens.json               all tokens; feeds dev/gentokens
components/<Name>/        README.md = the spec; preview.html = how it looks
fonts/                    IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono (OFL), to embed
assets/app-icon/          app icon (light, dark, mono)
assets/tray-icon/         tray glyph (black, white, active)
assets/tool-icons/        icon per tool, light and dark
assets/process-icon/      default process icons (masks)
assets/glyphs/            Material Symbols SVGs (Apache-2.0), interface glyphs
assets/screens/           reference screens from the original mockups
preview/index.html        every component, light and dark side by side

The brand book and specs say "the Glyphs asset group" etc.: that is assets/glyphs/, assets/tool-icons/, and so on.
